<p style="text-align: justify;">Greetings! I'm <b>Saleh Ahmed Shafin</b>, but most people call me <b>Shafin</b>. I’m from <b>Dhaka, Bangladesh</b>, and I currently work as an <b>AI Engineer</b> at <b>Codemen Solutions Limited</b>. I graduated with a degree in Computer Science and Engineering. My passion lies in learning new technologies and conducting research, specializing in <b>agentic AI systems</b>, <b>multi-agent orchestration</b>, and using my knowledge to solve complex engineering problems.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">In my current role at Codemen Solutions Limited, I architected <b>Alex</b>, a multi-agent Lead Orchestrator that autonomously routes complex requests across 16 specialized skill agents to serve global enterprise clients. I have extensive experience building advanced <b>LLM/RAG pipelines</b> (Vector, Graph, and Hybrid search using LangChain and LlamaIndex) for business-document intelligence, automated PO processing, and functional software prototype generation. By developing persistent memory systems like <b>Lore</b>, I help AI agents retain context over thousands of sessions. I work heavily with <b>FastAPI</b>, <b>Django</b>, <b>Python</b>, and various Vector/Graph databases, using <b>Docker</b> and <b>AWS</b> for seamless deployment while collaborating with a global engineering team.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">Before transitioning fully into AI engineering, I worked as a <b>Premium Freelance Marketer</b> at <b>EGEN</b>, where I gained expertise in <b>SEO</b>, <b>content marketing</b>, and <b>data analysis</b>. This experience enhanced my technical acumen and strengthened my adaptability in diverse professional environments.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">Throughout my journey, I have participated in several hackathons and datathons, achieving notable success. Recently, I secured <b>4th place</b> in the <b>Nascenia AI Hackathon</b> (Bengali Medical Dialogue Generation) and <b>5th place</b> in the <b>IUT Bengali.AI</b> (ASR for Regional Dialects). My previous achievements include <b>4th place</b> in the <b>ML Olympiad - CO2 Emissions Prediction Challenge</b>, <b>10th place</b> in the <b>DU DataVerse Challenge - ITVerse 2023</b>, <b>16th place</b> in the <b>BUET DL Sprint 2.0</b>, and <b>23rd place</b> in the <b>Robi Datathon 3.0</b>. These experiences have significantly sharpened my problem-solving abilities.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">I have a strong interest in the intersection of <b>Artificial Intelligence</b>, <b>Computer Vision</b>, <b>NLP</b>, and <b>Explainable AI (XAI)</b>. From building multimodal event identification systems to utilizing deep neural networks for crop and lung disease detection, I am fascinated by how ML and Deep Learning can solve real-world problems. I even recently published research on <i>Explainable Lung Disease Classification from Chest X-Ray Images</i>.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">I am continuously exploring new ways to stay at the forefront of AI technology. Additionally, I am open to <b>mentoring</b>, <b>creating educational content</b>, and would love to connect with anyone interested in collaborating on exciting opportunities.</p>
